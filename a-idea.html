A-IDEA

PROJECT DESCRIPTION
In this brand identity for a conference dedicated to showcasing artificial intelligence, my goal was to capture the difference between human and artificial intelligence approaches, what they would look like as user interfaces and how they can work together.
HUMAN CONTROL
a manual, almost touchable surface with buttons and physical interface elements
ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
a light, soft, ever-changing and adaptable design system

By definition: "technology that enables computers and machines to stimulate human learning, comprehension, problem solving, decision making, creativity and autonomy."

(SOURCE: "What is artificial intelligence?" by Cole Stryker, Eda Kavlakoglu)
DEEP LEARNING
"Deep learning is a subset of machine learning that uses multilayered neural networks, called deep neural networks, that more closely simulate the complex decision-making power of the human brain."

(SOURCE: "What is artificial intelligence?" by Cole Stryker, Eda Kavlakoglu)
THE FACE OF THE UKNOWN
a strange, uncanny "friend", an assistant, a robot. Something close to human.
