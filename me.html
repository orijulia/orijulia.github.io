Hi, my name is Julia, I am a designer with a diploma in Graphic Design BA (Moholy-Nagy University of Art and Design) and currently working on an MA diploma in Interaction Design (at the very same institution). I love graphic design with all my heart: mainly elegant, futuristic design with bright colors and tasteful fonts and shapes, while having something still unique in the process.


However, I always had this feeling that I want to put my design knowledge and style to good use. I seek to combine aesthetics and function to further elevate visual culture, emphasizing that pretty design is one thing: combined with smart design, we can dream big and help each other at the same time.


This is why my dream is to become part of a team that helps people and the environment, that thinks of sustainable and non-harming ways to elevate our quality of living, thinking of solutions that do not destroy but emphasize and reinforce. I consider myself a visual translator, an eye that sees shape and function, a pusher that seeks to find whatever she needs to help others. I also feel like I have to surround myself with people who think differently and know different things than I do, in order for us to think together for a greater achievement.
